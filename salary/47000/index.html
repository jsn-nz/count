FAQs

What are the total deductions on $47,000 in NZ?

For 2026–27: PAYE income tax is $7,133.00 and the ACC earners’ levy is $822.50 — $7,955.50 in total, an effective rate of 16.9%. Add 3.5% KiwiSaver ($1,645.00) and total deductions are $9,600.50 a year.

What is $47,000 a year per week after tax?

$719.22 a week with 3.5% KiwiSaver, or $750.86 a week with no KiwiSaver (PAYE and ACC only).

What are student loan repayments on a $47,000 salary?

Repayments are 12% of income above the $24,128 annual threshold: $2,744.64 a year ($52.78 a week), deducted through the M SL tax code.

Can I get the independent earner tax credit (IETC) on $47,000?

Possibly — the IETC is worth up to $520 a year for people earning between $24,000 and $70,000 who don’t receive Working for Families, a main benefit or NZ Super. The figures on this page (and the Count. calculator) exclude the IETC, so treat it as a possible bonus on top.

$47,000 a year is how much an hour in NZ?

At 40 hours a week, $47,000 a year is $22.60 an hour before tax. After PAYE and ACC you keep about $18.77 an hour ($17.98 with 3.5% KiwiSaver).
