Who gets which rate

  • Adult ($23.95): most employees aged 16 and over.
  • Starting-out ($19.16): some 16 to 19 year olds in their first six months with an employer, or who are doing industry training.
  • Training ($19.16): employees aged 20 or over doing at least 60 credits a year of recognised industry training.

The minimum wage applies to every hour worked, whether you are paid by the hour, by salary or by piece rate. Casual workers paid 8% holiday pay on top receive it in addition to the minimum.

FAQs

What is the minimum wage in NZ in 2026?

$23.95 an hour for adults from 1 April 2026. The starting-out and training minimum wages are $19.16 an hour (80% of the adult rate).

What is minimum wage after tax for a 40-hour week?

$958.00 gross becomes $794.58 a week after PAYE and the ACC levy, or $761.06 a week with 3.5% KiwiSaver.

What is the minimum wage as a yearly salary?

$49,816 a year for 40 hours a week, 52 weeks a year, or $41,318.42 after PAYE and ACC.

Can a salaried employee be paid below minimum wage?

No. A salary must work out to at least the minimum wage for every hour actually worked in each pay period.
