How lump sums are taxed

Bonuses, back pay, cashed-up leave, retirement payments and redundancy are “extra pays”. Instead of using the normal PAYE tables, your employer:

  • takes your regular gross pay for the last four weeks and multiplies it by 13 to estimate a yearly income (or the last month multiplied by 12 if you are paid monthly),
  • adds the lump sum, and
  • taxes the whole lump sum at one flat rate, the rate for the band that total falls in.

The ACC earners’ levy (1.75%) is added while your annualised income is under $156,641. This calculator uses your salary as the annualised figure; if your last four weeks were unusually high or low, your employer’s number will differ.

Example: a $5,000 bonus on an $80,000 salary. The total of $85,000 is in the $78,101 – $180,000 band, so $1,650.00 PAYE is withheld, plus $87.50 ACC.

Redundancy payments

Redundancy is taxed as a lump sum using the same flat-rate method, with two differences: no ACC earners’ levy is charged and no KiwiSaver is deducted. Student loan repayments are still deducted. Notice pay and unused annual leave paid out with it are ordinary taxable pay.

Why a bonus feels over-taxed

The flat rate treats the whole lump sum as your top slice of income, which is usually right. It over-taxes you when the lump sum straddles a bracket threshold or your annualised pay overstates what you really earn in the year. Inland Revenue recalculates after 31 March and refunds any excess automatically.

FAQs

How much tax do I pay on a bonus in NZ?

A flat rate of 10.5%, 17.5%, 30.0%, 33.0%, 39.0%, depending on your annualised income plus the bonus, with the 1.75% ACC levy on top. On an $80,000 salary a $5,000 bonus is taxed at 33.0%.

Is redundancy pay taxed in NZ?

Yes. It is taxed as a lump sum at the flat extra-pay rate. There is no tax-free amount, but the ACC earners’ levy and KiwiSaver are not deducted from it.

Are bonuses taxed more than normal pay?

No. Over the full year a bonus is taxed like any other income. The flat-rate method can withhold too much at the time, and the excess is refunded after the tax year ends.

Does KiwiSaver come out of my bonus?

Usually yes, at your normal contribution rate, unless your employment agreement says the bonus is discretionary and excluded. It is not deducted from redundancy.
