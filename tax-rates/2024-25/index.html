FAQs

What are the NZ income tax rates for 2024–25?

10.5% on $0 – $14,000; 12.82% on $14,001 – $15,600; 17.5% on $15,601 – $48,000; 21.64% on $48,001 – $53,500; 30.0% on $53,501 – $70,000; 30.99% on $70,001 – $78,100; 33.0% on $78,101 – $180,000; 39.0% on over $180,000.

When does the 2024–25 tax year start and end?

It runs from 1 April 2024 to 31 March 2025.

What is the top tax rate in New Zealand?

39%, on income over $180,000.

What was the ACC earners' levy in 2024–25?

1.60% of earnings up to $142,283.
