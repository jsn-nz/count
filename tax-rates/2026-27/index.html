FAQs

What are the NZ income tax rates for 2026–27?

10.5% on $0 – $15,600; 17.5% on $15,601 – $53,500; 30.0% on $53,501 – $78,100; 33.0% on $78,101 – $180,000; 39.0% on over $180,000.

When does the 2026–27 tax year start and end?

It runs from 1 April 2026 to 31 March 2027.

What is the top tax rate in New Zealand?

39%, on income over $180,000.

What was the ACC earners' levy in 2026–27?

1.75% of earnings up to $156,641.
