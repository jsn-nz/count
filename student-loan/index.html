How repayments work

If you have a New Zealand student loan and use a tax code ending in SL, your employer deducts 12 cents for every dollar you earn above $24,128 a year. That is $464 a week or $928 a fortnight before tax. Nothing is deducted in a pay period where you earn less than the threshold.

Repayments come out of your pay alongside PAYE and go straight to Inland Revenue. While you live in New Zealand the loan is interest-free, so every dollar repaid reduces the balance.

Second jobs and going overseas

  • A second job on an SL code repays 12% of every dollar, with no threshold. See secondary tax.
  • You can make extra repayments at any time through myIR or internet banking.
  • If you move overseas for six months or more the loan generally starts to attract interest and repayments are set by your loan balance instead of your income. Check with IRD before you go.

FAQs

What is the student loan repayment threshold in NZ for 2026–27?

$24,128 a year ($464 a week). You repay 12% of income above that.

How much student loan do I repay on $70,000?

$5,504.64 a year, which is $105.86 a week: 12% of the $45,872 you earn above the $24,128 threshold.

Is there interest on NZ student loans?

Not while you live in New Zealand. Borrowers based overseas are generally charged interest.

Do KiwiSaver contributions reduce my student loan repayments?

No. Repayments are worked out on gross pay, before KiwiSaver is taken out.
